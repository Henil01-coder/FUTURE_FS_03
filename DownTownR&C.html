About Restro & Café

Downtown Restro & Café is a family-owned local business serving the community with love since 2015. Our mission is to create a warm, welcoming space where everyone can enjoy premium coffee, delicious food, and genuine smiles.

We partner with local roasters and bakers to ensure the freshest ingredients, support other small businesses, and care for the planet with eco-friendly practices.

Stop in for a cup, a chat, or to catch up on some work—we can't wait to serve you!
